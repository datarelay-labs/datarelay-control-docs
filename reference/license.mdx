## Permitted during Early Access

You may use DataRelay Control free of charge for:

- personal use
- education and non-commercial research
- evaluation, testing, lab, and proof-of-concept use
- your organization's own internal business operations
- internal source-code modification for permitted use
- customer-owned deployments where the customer directly controls and operates DataRelay Control
- paid installation, integration, migration, maintenance, training, or support around a customer-owned deployment, provided DataRelay Control itself is not resold or operated as a managed service

## Requires a separate commercial license

Without a separate written commercial license from DataRelay Labs, you may not:

- resell or sublicense DataRelay Control
- commercially redistribute DataRelay Control or a modified version as a standalone product
- OEM, embed, bundle, or white-label DataRelay Control in another commercial product or appliance
- rebrand or fork DataRelay Control for commercial productization
- provide DataRelay Control functionality as SaaS, MSP, hosted, shared, managed, or multi-tenant service
- use DataRelay Control or substantial portions of its source code to create, improve, or maintain a competing or substitute commercial product
- remove or obscure copyright, license, attribution, or trademark notices
- describe DataRelay Control as OSI-approved open-source software

## Commercial General Availability

DataRelay Labs intends to move DataRelay Control to Commercial General Availability after the Early Access period and product-stability qualification.

The planned commercial model is:

- **Perpetual Software License**
- self-hosted and offline-capable operation
- no subscription required merely to keep a licensed version running
- optional or separately licensed maintenance and support
- commercial SKU and entitlement limits defined when Commercial General Availability is announced

Future Commercial General Availability releases may require a commercial license and may use license terms different from the Early Access License.

The Early Access License applies to copies and versions distributed with that license. It does not grant access, upgrade, maintenance, support, or licensing rights to future releases.

## Support during Early Access

Early Access is intended for real-world use, validation, testing, feedback, and product stabilization.

Unless DataRelay Labs separately agrees in writing:

- no SLA is provided
- support is best effort
- features, interfaces, packaging, compatibility, and documentation may change between Early Access releases
- operators are responsible for validating suitability for their environment

## Why Source Available?

The source code is publicly visible so users can inspect, evaluate, test, and make permitted internal modifications.

The license restricts resale, commercial productization, competing products, and SaaS/MSP/hosted use. DataRelay Control is therefore **Source Available, not Open Source**.
